Zeplee

File sharing, live now

Share files without the friction. Send a file as a link or open a shared space, with no sign-up and nothing to install.

  • Files up to 10 GB without an account, on links that expire after 24 hours, 3 days or never.
  • Shared spaces where people drop files, write notes and sketch on a whiteboard together.
  • Password-protected spaces, custom links and QR codes for moving files between devices.

Cowatch

Watch parties, live now

Watch anything, together. One link opens a shared cloud browser, so friends can watch Netflix, YouTube or Twitch in sync.

  • A shared virtual browser streamed to everyone in the room, with one person driving playback.
  • Built-in voice and live chat, so nobody needs a separate call running.
  • No downloads and no account to join. Share a link and press play.
