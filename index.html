01 The short version

I'm a back-end engineer who treats server-side code like craft, not plumbing.

Over four years I've shipped the kind of software that doesn't get screenshots on dribbble but quietly runs entire businesses — audit platforms, risk management systems for major Gulf enterprises, and a fintech product getting ready for launch. .NET sits at the core, Flutter is my second blade for when the backend needs a face, and cloud wraps around all of it. I'm not precious about languages either — hand me a new framework and I'll be productive in it before the coffee goes cold.

When I'm not on product work, I'm building tools: published NuGet packages, VS Code extensions, and internal utilities that make colleagues' day jobs measurably less annoying. If something gets done manually twice, I'll automate it the third time.
